## 🚀 Featured Projects

### Aurah AI

Nigeria's first **decision-intelligence platform**, designed to combine artificial intelligence with live human expertise. Users describe their financial, legal, or business challenges in plain language, and Aurah AI provides immediate AI-driven insights before seamlessly connecting them with vetted professionals whenever human expertise is needed. The platform eliminates unnecessary delays, repeated explanations, and expensive consultation barriers, making expert advice faster and more accessible.

### ALU_Intern

A platform built to bridge the gap between **African Leadership University students** and startups, organizations, and internship providers. ALU_Intern centralizes internship opportunities, simplifies applications, and helps students discover career opportunities while enabling startups to connect with talented students.

### Injiza

A **USSD-based bookkeeping solution** designed for local businesses and entrepreneurs in Rwanda. Injiza enables users without smartphones or reliable internet access to record sales, expenses, and business transactions through simple USSD menus, making financial record-keeping accessible to everyone.

A full-stack e-commerce platform that empowers entrepreneurs and small businesses by providing a modern online marketplace with secure product management, scalable backend architecture, and integrated APIs.

### Pill Identifier
